This interactive map shows parcels in Kenton County, color-coded by suitability score. Unbuildable lands are excluded from the suitability layer. Each parcel's score is based on the following considerations: Access to amenities and transit, walkability, rates of car-less commuting, and existing infrastructure (water and sewer).
